## 1. New Client Welcome Sequence

### Email 1 of 3 -- Welcome + What to Expect

**Trigger:** Immediately after first booking is confirmed
**Target:** New clients (first-time bookers)
**Subject:** You're booked. Here's what happens next.
**Preview text:** Your shoot is locked in. A few things to know before we show up.

---

{{contact.first_name}},

Welcome to Avery & Bryant.

You just made the best decision for your listing. We shoot for 200+ agents across Central Arkansas, and we treat every property like it's a $2M listing. Yours is no different.

**Here's what happens from here:**

1. We show up on time. Always.
2. We shoot your property in roughly 60-90 minutes depending on size.
3. Your edited photos are delivered within 24 hours via your Aryeo gallery -- download-ready, MLS-ready, social-ready.

**How to prep the property before we arrive:**

- **Lights on.** Every single one. Lamps, overheads, under-cabinet -- all of them.
- **Blinds open.** Let the natural light in.
- **Counters clear.** Kitchen and bathrooms especially. Remove personal items, pet bowls, trash cans.
- **Beds made.** Pillows fluffed. Throws folded.
- **Garage doors closed.** Exterior shots look cleaner.
- **Lawn mowed.** Curb appeal matters more than you think.
- **Cars out of the driveway.** We need a clean approach shot.
- **Toilet lids down.** Seriously. Every time.

The better the property looks when we arrive, the better your photos look online. We handle the angles, the lighting, the edit. You handle the prep.

If anything changes with your booking, just reply to this email.

**Thomas Brown**
Avery & Bryant
Little Rock, AR
BBB A+ Rated | 5-Star Google Reviews
