## 4 · Revenue impact estimate

Based on the matrix + current mix assumptions (real estate listings ~60% of revenue, multi-family / builders / airbnb ~25%, studio + branding ~15%):

**Net expected effect on average order value: +35% on new orders** driven by:
- Real estate photo tier increases (the biggest volume category) lift base orders
- Multi-Family and Airbnb moving into realistic pricing
- Video + drone standalone prices rising from below-market
- Branding Content Day unlocks high-ticket revenue that didn't exist as a product
- Commercial opens a new revenue channel entirely

Offsetting the increases: builder retainer, floor plans, virtual twilight — all **coming down**. Those are corrective. They were pricing A&B out of deals.

**Break-even math on the member tiers:** customer hits cost-recovery around 6–7 hours of studio use per month. Margin stays healthy at every tier.
