# Avery & Bryant Pricing Matrix

**Goal:** Price every service across every vertical at a level that is **premium but approachable** — top 25% of the Arkansas / Midsouth market, with ~25% headroom below luxury-only providers.

**Built:** 2026-04-18. Research-backed. Revise quarterly.

---

## Methodology

### Research source
Market benchmarks pulled 2026-04-18 from:
- **Aryeo 2024-2025 Real Estate Photography Industry Reports** (national median rates, heavy weight — Aryeo hosts many of the photographers we compete against)
- **PFRE (Photography for Real Estate)** forums + annual rate surveys
- **RES (Real Estate Staging Association)** for virtual staging benchmarks
- **Wholesale suppliers** (BoxBrownie, CubiCasa, VirtualStagingAI, RetouchUp) for cost-plus math on commoditized services
- **Regional rate cards** from Memphis, Nashville, and Arkansas photographers (HomeJab affiliates, TruPlace, LCP Media, Planomatic for multi-family)

### Regional adjustment
National mid-market rates × ~85–90% = Arkansas/Midsouth midpoint. Luxury markets (LA, NYC, Miami) run 60–80% above Arkansas baseline. We position at the top of the Midsouth band, not luxury.

### Confidence levels
- **STRONG** — photos, drone add-on, virtual staging, twilight, 3D tours, CRE, branding headshots (lots of public data)
- **MEDIUM** — multi-family, builder retainers, rendered 3D floor plans, social reels
- **THIN** — lot/land with boundary overlays (niche; Arkansas-specific call)

### Pricing tiers (what the numbers represent)
- **Starter** (DIY/budget competitors — we don't play here)
- **Mid-market** (typical regional photographer)
- **Premium-approachable** (our target — top 25% but not luxury-only)
- **Luxury** (boutique, high-touch, $2k+ per shoot)

## The Spot — Membership model (FINAL, 2026-04-20)

Credits do **not** equate to studio hours. Studio time is billed at a
member-discounted rate; credits are reserved for add-ons.

| Tier | Monthly | Studio discount | Add-on credits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Creator Lite | $60/mo | **10% off** all studio time | 4 credits/mo |
| Creator | $100/mo | **20% off** all studio time | 8 credits/mo |
| Pro | $180/mo | **30% off** all studio time + priority booking | 16 credits/mo |

**Member discount applies to:** Podcast Room (1hr/2hr/half-day), Alternate Set,
The Garage, Multi-Set Day Pass.

**Credits redeemable for:** editing rounds, engineer assist, equipment access,
extra 30-min blocks, rush delivery. Resets monthly (no rollover).

**Break-even math:**
- Creator Lite: ~7 hrs studio/mo at full rate vs. $60 membership cost
- Creator: ~6 hrs studio/mo
- Pro: ~7 hrs studio/mo

Heavy users get most value. Members save 10–30% on every booking, and the
add-on credits are effectively free workflow support baked into the subscription.

## Key Moves (ranked by impact)

### 🟢 High-impact increases (ship these first — biggest upside, low risk)
1. **Cinematic Video $250 → $695** — 178% increase but correctly priced. Video is already priced bundled; standalone buyers are currently getting a gift.
2. **Branding Headshots $200 → $449** — 125% increase. Nashville/Memphis brand photographers routinely charge $400-$800. $200 signals "amateur."
3. **Real Estate photo tiers +50% across the board** — not a single jump; phase in (see migration below).
4. **Multi-Family Leasing Essentials $650 → $895** — biggest multifamily lift, matches actual shoot time and deliverables.
5. **Add a Content Day tier to Branding ($1,495)** — this is where agents actually want to spend; the current offer tops out at $500 with no on-ramp to retainer.

### 🔴 High-impact decreases (fix bleeders — these are actively costing deals)
1. **Builder Progress Program $599/mo → $325/mo** — 46% down. Currently 2x market; probably why few builders have signed up.
2. **Builder Single Visit $400 → $295** — 31% down. Inflates the intro and loses leads.
3. **2D Floor Plan $135 → $79** — commoditized; $135 is a friction point on add-on sheet.
4. **Virtual Twilight $90 → $49** — AI tools have collapsed cost; $90 reads "overcharging."

### 🟡 Hold or tweak
- PRO and PRO+ packages: tweak by $25-50, don't rebuild
- Multi-Family Full Property and Marketing Suite: reasonable as-is
- Virtual Staging at $65/img: **hold** if we market as "designer-level" vs. generic AI; otherwise drop to $49
- Lot & Land base tier: raise slightly ($175 → $199)

## Migration Strategy (don't raise everything at once)

Raising all prices simultaneously = repeat-client sticker shock. Raising incrementally = margin creep with low churn.

### Phase 1 — ship immediately (low-risk, high-upside)
- Commercial launch pricing (no existing clients, no migration pain)
- Branding Content Day tier launch
- Cinematic Video add-on: $250 → $495 (midway; full $695 in phase 2)
- Drone standalone: $125 → $149 (midway)
- Builder Progress Program: $599 → $395/mo (midway; full $325 in phase 2)
- 2D Floor Plan: $135 → $99 (midway)

### Phase 2 — 45-60 days after Phase 1 metrics review
Look at conversion rate on each service. If volume held:
- Cinematic Video $495 → $695
- Drone $149 → $199
- Builder Progress $395 → $325
- 2D Floor Plan $99 → $79
- Start sqft-tier increases on Real Estate photos (midway)

### Phase 3 — 90 days total, after second review
- Full sqft tier increases on Real Estate photos
- Multi-Family Leasing Essentials $650 → $895
- Branding Headshots $200 → $449 (can be done sooner if market reaction is soft)
- Virtual Twilight $90 → $49
- Retire any package that never sells

### Phase 4 — grandfathering
- Existing repeat clients (tagged `repeat-client` or `client monthly` in GHL) get a 90-day grandfather period on their package price
- After 90 days, they move to new pricing with a 10% loyalty discount for the first renewal

## Open pricing questions (decide before shipping)

1. **Virtual Staging positioning** — hold at $65/img as "designer-level" or drop to $49 to match market? Answer affects competitive positioning.
2. **Branding Team Day scaling** — is the base $1,995 for up to 5, or 3? Need to define scaling formula.
3. **Commercial Dealership retainer** — $895/mo is a starting proposal. What's the deliverable bundle (lot drone cadence, inventory count, showroom passes)? Need a concrete offer to quote.
4. **3D Tour (Matterport) — hold or drop?** Residential 3D tours are declining market-wide because Zillow 3D Home is free. Do we keep it or push clients to Zillow?
5. **Lot & Land Vision Package ($895+)** — AI rendering via StudioAI is a real differentiator. Is the pricing defensible? Could we charge $1,295 for complex renders?

## Data confidence disclosure

Prices flagged below were set on thinner data — adjust by tasting:

- **Multi-Family pricing** (MEDIUM confidence) — based on LCP Media + Planomatic rate cards. Arkansas multi-family photography market is smaller; there's judgment in these numbers.
- **Lot & Land with boundary overlays** (THIN confidence) — niche. Arkansas timber/hunting land market has its own dynamic we should validate with a few sample quotes.
- **Builder retainer** (MEDIUM confidence) — highly negotiated in practice. The $325/mo number is the midpoint; actual quotes will vary.
- **Branding Content Day / Team Day** (MEDIUM confidence) — these are new tiers A&B doesn't currently offer; market data is from Nashville/Memphis brand photographers.

## Source mapping back to the form spec

This matrix feeds into [`docs/order-form-field-specs.md`](./order-form-field-specs.md):

- Each Aryeo form's "Packages" step uses the vertical's package prices from the table above
- Each Aryeo form's "À la carte" step uses the standard-services + premium/add-on prices for that vertical column
- "Add-ons" step uses premium/add-on services that aren't already in the package being selected
- GHL custom field `Services Selected` (multi-options) tracks which à la carte items landed in the order for upsell logic

When Aryeo forms are rebuilt, use this matrix as the pricing source of truth. Wholesale costs (e.g. BoxBrownie $4-8/image for day-to-dusk, CubiCasa $30-80 for 2D floor plans) are NOT to be marked up below these retail rates without discussion — our margin floor is "retail ≥ 3× wholesale" for commoditized services.
