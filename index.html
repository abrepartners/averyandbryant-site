FAQ

How long does setup take?▾
48 hours or less. We start with a 15-minute discovery call to understand your business, then our team builds your custom AI agent. You're live within two business days.
Can callers tell they're talking to AI?▾
Most can't. Answr uses natural language processing trained on real business conversations. We customize the voice, personality, and terminology to match your brand.
What if there's an urgent call?▾
Answr detects urgency and transfers those calls directly to you or your designated team member. You set the rules for what counts as urgent.
Do I need technical knowledge?▾
Zero. This is done-for-you. We handle all setup, configuration, and ongoing optimization. You just tell us how you want calls handled.

About

Built by an operator.
Not a spectator.

Avery & Bryant started as a real estate media company. Now we're building the AI infrastructure that the entire industry will run on.

Thomas Brown is a U.S. Army veteran who built Avery & Bryant from the ground up in Arkansas. What started as listing photography evolved into something much bigger when Thomas realized the real estate industry's technology gap wasn't just an inconvenience. It was a competitive moat waiting to be built.

Today, ALYT operates four integrated service lines: AI communications (Answr), AI-powered listing media (StudioAI), traditional media production, and brand content systems (Creative Studio). Every product is designed with one principle: build it once, automate it, and let it scale without adding headcount.

We're not a software reseller. We're not a virtual assistant company. We're a real estate AI infrastructure company that builds proprietary technology and integrates it into the daily operations of agents, brokerages, and property managers.
